How This Works
1 Click Authorize — Intuit's secure login page opens
2 Sign in with the QBO company's credentials and grant permission
3 We fetch the real company name from QuickBooks automatically
4 You land directly in the company workspace — ready to go
5 Run a Sync, then an Assessment to start AI bookkeeping
Your QBO password is never entered into or stored by this app. OAuth 2.0 only. Tokens are encrypted. Each company is fully isolated by Realm ID. No QBO writes are performed during connection.
